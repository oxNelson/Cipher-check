    How this score is calculated

    The 0–100 score adds length (0–40), character categories (0–20), unique characters (0–15), and a pattern-resistance component (0–25). Detected weaknesses reduce the pattern-resistance component. It is a rule-based comparison, not a probability or estimate of attack time.

    Bands: Very Weak 0–19; Weak 20–39; Fair 40–64; Strong 65–84; Very Strong 85–100. The rules are documented in the README.

    CipherCheck does not check breach databases. No findings does not guarantee that a password is safe.
